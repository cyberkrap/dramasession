Obsession Fan Club

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 20, 2026

This policy explains the information used to operate and protect the Obsession Fan Club platform. The platform is intended only for users aged 18 or older.

1. Information you provide

Depending on the features you use, you may provide an account name, login credentials, email address, profile information, posts, comments, chat messages, votes, uploaded media, reports, support requests, and communications with administrators.

Content submitted to public areas may be visible to other users and visitors. Do not post personal or sensitive information that you do not want others to see, copy, archive, or share.

2. Information collected automatically

When you use the platform, servers and security systems may process technical information such as IP address, approximate network location, browser and device type, operating system, referring page, requested URLs, timestamps, session identifiers, cookie data, error logs, and activity needed to detect abuse or maintain the service.

3. How information is used

  • Provide accounts, authentication, posts, comments, chat, voting, uploads, awards, and other platform functions.
  • Maintain security, prevent fraud, detect alternate-account abuse, investigate reports, and enforce Community Rules.
  • Respond to account, moderation, privacy, legal, and support requests.
  • Monitor reliability, diagnose errors, measure feature usage, and improve the platform.
  • Process optional support benefits and confirm transaction status where applicable.
  • Comply with lawful requests and protect the rights, safety, and integrity of users and the platform.

4. Cookies and local storage

The platform uses cookies or similar browser storage for essential functions such as keeping you signed in, protecting forms, remembering preferences, maintaining security, and supporting site features. Blocking essential cookies may prevent parts of the platform from working correctly.

5. Public activity

Usernames, profiles, posts, comments, votes, awards, badges, moderation records, and other community activity may be public depending on the feature and your settings. Public information may remain in screenshots, search indexes, caches, archives, quotations, or copies made by others even after it is edited or removed from the platform.

6. Service providers and disclosures

Information may be processed by infrastructure, hosting, security, email, analytics, storage, content-delivery, and payment providers that help operate the platform. These providers may process information under their own contractual and privacy obligations.

Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with law or valid legal process, investigate fraud or security incidents, enforce platform rules, protect users, defend legal rights, or respond to an emergency involving safety.

7. Payments

Payment providers process payment credentials under their own privacy policies. The platform may receive limited information such as transaction identifiers, payment status, support tier, and account details needed to provide or manage the related benefit. Do not send payment-card details through posts, chat, or the Contact form.

8. Data retention

Information is retained for as long as reasonably needed to operate the platform, maintain records, prevent abuse, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, satisfy legal obligations, and preserve security. Retention periods vary by data type and purpose. Some records may remain in backups or security logs after deletion from active pages.

9. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used to protect platform information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Use a unique password, protect your account, and promptly report suspected unauthorized access through the Contact page.

10. Your choices and requests

You may update available account settings and contact administrators to request help with account access, correction, deletion, or privacy concerns. A request may require identity or account verification. Some information may be retained where necessary for security, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or enforcement.

11. Adults only

The platform is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly invite minors to create accounts or submit personal information. Accounts reasonably believed to belong to minors may be restricted or removed.

12. International processing

The platform, its operators, and service providers may process information in countries different from your own. Data-protection rules may vary between locations. By using the platform, you understand that information may be transferred and processed where the service and its providers operate.

13. Third-party links and embedded content

Links, embedded media, and integrations may connect to third-party services. Those services control their own data practices, and this Privacy Policy does not govern them. Review their policies before providing information.

14. Changes to this Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the platform, technology, or legal requirements change. The revised policy becomes effective when posted with a new update date.

15. Contact

Use the Contact page for privacy questions, account-data requests, security reports, or concerns about information displayed on the platform.
