Submit a Hat

Create an avatar overlay using the official canvas and credit the person who actually made it.

Submission rules

Hats are small overlays, so technical accuracy matters.

  1. Start with the 100 x 130 hat template. Do not submit an image larger than 100 x 130 pixels.
  2. The circle in the template represents the profile picture. Do not include that circle in the final image.
  3. Use a transparent background. A solid rectangle covering the avatar will be rejected.
  4. Keep the important artwork inside the canvas and test that it still reads clearly over a profile picture.
  5. No AI-generated or AI-edited submissions. Do not submit stolen artwork or another user's work without permission.
  6. Use a clear name and a short description that explains what the hat is, not a joke or unrelated message.
  7. Credit the actual creator. Your account is filled in automatically, but you may change it when submitting for someone else.
  8. Moderators may adjust the name, description, or price, and may reject duplicate, broken, unsafe, or low-quality submissions.
