Submit an Emote

Upload an emote, credit its actual creator, and add only the search terms people would realistically use.

Submission rules

These are review requirements, not suggestions.

  1. Submit something people could use naturally in posts or chat. Extremely specific reaction images belong elsewhere.
  2. The subject must remain recognisable at normal emote size. Tiny text, screenshots, and cluttered compositions usually fail.
  3. Use a transparent background unless the background is essential to the image.
  4. No AI-generated or AI-edited submissions.
  5. Use a clear, correctly spelled name. When a similar name already exists, number it sequentially instead of misspelling it.
  6. Tags exist for search. Use a few relevant, single-word tags. Do not add jokes, filler, or phrases containing spaces.
  7. Do not include your username as a tag. Your work is credited automatically and searching @{{v.username}} finds emotes credited to you.
  8. Moderators may rename, retag, reject, or remove submissions that are duplicate, low-quality, unsafe, or unsuitable for the picker.
