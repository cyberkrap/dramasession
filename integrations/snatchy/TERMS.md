# Snatchy Terms of Use

Last updated: August 14, 2026

Snatchy is a community bridge operated for The Obsession Club. Its purpose is to mirror new submissions from r/obsessionmovie into /b/obsession on The Obsession Club (TOC).

## What Snatchy does

Snatchy creates a TOC thread from a new Reddit submission, credits the original Reddit author, links the original submission, and may copy self-text and media/link URLs supplied by Reddit's Devvit trigger.

Snatchy does not import Reddit comments and does not post TOC comments back to Reddit.

## Independent moderation

Reddit and TOC are separate communities with separate moderation decisions. A moderator removal on Reddit does not automatically remove the TOC thread. TOC moderators may moderate the TOC thread under TOC's own rules.

If the Reddit author deletes the source submission, Snatchy scrubs the copied Reddit content and source attribution from the TOC post while preserving TOC-native discussion.

## Availability

The bridge is provided as-is and may be interrupted, delayed, disabled, or changed due to Reddit, Devvit, TOC, network, moderation, or technical conditions. No guarantee is made that every eligible Reddit event will be delivered or mirrored immediately.

## Acceptable use

Snatchy must not be used to bypass Reddit access controls, impersonate Reddit users, infer that similarly named Reddit and TOC accounts belong to the same person, or obtain private Reddit data.

## Changes

These terms may be updated when Snatchy's behavior or platform requirements change. The current version is maintained with the Snatchy source code.
